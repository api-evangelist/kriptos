# Kriptos

> Kriptos is an AI-driven unstructured data security platform that discovers, classifies, and labels documents automatically across endpoints, servers, and cloud storage, then feeds that business and risk context to the DLP, CASB, SIEM, EDR and IAM tools an organization already runs. Founded in Ecuador and backed by Techstars, it serves banking, insurance, healthcare and utilities customers primarily across Latin America.

## Platform

- [Data Discovery & Classification](https://www.kriptos.io/data-discovery-classification): Automated discovery and multi-model AI classification of unstructured data across all environments, producing a continuous inventory.
- [Data Compliance & Risk Management](https://www.kriptos.io/data-compliance-risk-management): Real-time inventory, PII detection, risk signals and compliance reporting over classified data.
- [Data Access Governance](https://www.kriptos.io/data-access-governance): Permission management and remediation of overexposure across Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace.
- [Insider Threat Management](https://www.kriptos.io/insider-threat-management): Correlates user behavior with data sensitivity, enriching SIEM alerts with UEBA context.
- [DSPM](https://www.kriptos.io/dspm): Data security posture management over unstructured content.
- [Platform Dashboard](https://www.kriptos.io/platform/dashboard): The Kriptos web platform.

## Solutions

- [Prevent Data Loss](https://www.kriptos.io/prevent-data-loss): Adds a classification intelligence layer to existing DLP and CASB rather than replacing them.
- [Secure AI Adoption](https://www.kriptos.io/secure-ai-adoption): Shadow-AI monitoring and prevention of sensitive data leaking into AI tools including Microsoft Copilot and Google Gemini.
- [Secure Microsoft 365](https://www.kriptos.io/secure-microsoft-365): Business-aware classification written back as native Microsoft Purview labels.
- [Secure Google Workspace](https://www.kriptos.io/enhance-google-workspace-protection): Native Google Drive labels driven by Kriptos classification.
